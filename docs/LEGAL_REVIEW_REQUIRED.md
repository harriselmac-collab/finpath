# Pocket Ahead release owner confirmations

The app and public web pages no longer contain unresolved legal placeholders. They currently identify **Kael Labs** as the publisher and use an effective date of **9 August 2026**.

Before Google Play submission, the account owner must still confirm:

1. **Publisher identity** — whether “Kael Labs” is the exact public publisher/trading name that should appear in the app and Play listing.
2. **Support and privacy contact** — a monitored email address for the Play listing and the `EXPO_PUBLIC_SUPPORT_EMAIL` production environment value.
3. **Legal review** — whether a registered business name, mailing address, or a specific governing-law clause is legally required for the publisher’s jurisdiction. The current terms preserve applicable mandatory consumer law without naming a jurisdiction.
4. **Retention language** — active app data is deleted immediately; limited security logs and encrypted provider backups may remain temporarily for fraud prevention, legal compliance, and backup rotation.

The policy and terms are a product-release draft, not a substitute for advice from qualified counsel in the publisher’s jurisdiction.
