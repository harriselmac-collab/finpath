Pocket Ahead · Kael Labs

Privacy policy

Version 1.0.0 · Effective 9 August 2026

1. Who controls your data

Pocket Ahead is published by Kael Labs. For privacy or data-rights requests, use the Contact support option in the app or the developer contact shown on the Pocket Ahead Google Play listing.

2. Data we process

We process your account identifier and email; optional profile and onboarding details; income, transactions, bills, debts, goals and contributions; language, currency, theme and notification preferences; and an optional profile image.

Religion or cultural-event preferences are optional and are processed only after explicit consent. Pocket Ahead does not sell personal data, show targeted advertising, connect to bank accounts, or send financial records to a generative-AI provider.

3. Why we process it

We use this data to calculate and display your plan, synchronize it across signed-in devices, protect your account, provide requested features, and respond to support or legal requests. Optional sensitive preferences rely on consent that can be withdrawn in the app.

4. Service providers and transfers

Supabase provides account authentication, database synchronization, file storage and account deletion infrastructure. If you choose Google sign-in, Google processes the authentication request. Android and Google Play may process technical data under their own terms. These providers may process data outside your country using their applicable safeguards.

5. Retention, export and deletion

Active account data is kept while your account is in use. From Profile you can export a machine-readable copy or permanently delete your account. Deletion removes active app records and authentication access; limited security logs and encrypted backups may remain temporarily for fraud prevention, legal compliance and provider backup rotation before expiry.

6. Your choices and security

You can correct profile information, change optional preferences, withdraw optional consent, export data, and delete the account. Pocket Ahead uses access controls, encrypted transport and secure device storage, but no service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Age and policy changes

Pocket Ahead is intended for adults aged 18 or over. Material changes will be reflected by an updated effective date and, when appropriate, an in-app notice.
