## 1. Metadata Fields

* **App Title**: FinPath — Personal Budget & Savings Planner
* **Short Description**: Track expenses, log debts, calculate surplus, and plan goals with Gemini AI.
* **Full Description**:
FinPath is a premium, secure budgeting companion built to help you master your cash flow, optimize debt repayments, and reach your savings milestones.

Features:
- Track income and categorize expenses.
- Monitor your debt repayment tracks (balances, interest rates, minimum payments).
- Set goals (Emergency Funds, custom targets) and contribute directly.
- Get secure, private, conversational explanations of your budget health powered by Google Gemini.
- Designed with GDPR compliance (Privacy Centre, JSON data export, account deletion).
