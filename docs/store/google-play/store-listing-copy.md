## Metadata

- **App title**: Pocket Ahead: Budget Plan
- **Short description**: Plan spending, track expenses, debts, bills and goals in nine languages.

## Full description

Pocket Ahead is a personal-finance planning app that helps you understand what your money needs to cover before your next income date.

Use Pocket Ahead to:

- Build a monthly plan from income and essential costs.
- Record expenses and income manually.
- Track recurring bills, debts, savings goals and upcoming costs.
- See a planning estimate for what may be safe to spend until your next income.
- Choose from multiple currencies and nine interface languages.
- Use the app locally without an account, or sign in to synchronize your plan across devices.
- Export your account data or delete your account from inside the app.

Pocket Ahead does not connect to bank accounts, hold or transfer money, offer credit, or provide investment, tax or professional financial advice. Its calculations are planning estimates based on information entered by the user.
