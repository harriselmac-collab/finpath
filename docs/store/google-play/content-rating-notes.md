# Google Play Content Rating Notes: FinPath

Content rating notes for the IARC questionnaire.

---

## 1. Classification Focus
* **App Category**: Utility / Finance.
* **Violence / Fear / Sex**: None.
* **User-Generated Content**: Users can enter custom goal names and transaction descriptions locally.
* **Data Sharing**: No user-entered text is shared with other users.
* **Physical Location**: Location coordinates are not shared.
* **Digital Purchases**: None (the app does not contain in-app purchases or subscription gates).
