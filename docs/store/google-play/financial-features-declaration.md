# Google Play Financial Features Declaration: Pocket Ahead

## Implemented features

- **Expense tracking**: Users manually record expenses and income.
- **Budget planning**: The app calculates deterministic planning estimates from user-entered income, costs and dates.
- **Debt tracking**: Users can record balances, interest rates and minimum payments.
- **Savings goals**: Users can create goals and record contributions.

## Not implemented

- Direct bank or open-banking connections.
- Money transfer, wallet or payment processing.
- Lending, credit decisions or loan brokerage.
- Investments, securities or cryptocurrency trading.
- Insurance products.
- Tax, legal or professional financial advice.
- Generative-AI financial explanations.

Pocket Ahead is a planning and record-keeping tool. It does not act as a financial institution and does not hold user funds.
