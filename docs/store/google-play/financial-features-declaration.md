# Google Play Financial Features Declaration: FinPath

FinPath provides expense tracking, budget planning, debt tracker tools, and personal savings goals. We must declare our exact financial feature coverage accurately to prevent store compliance violations.

---

## 1. Feature Coverage Declaration

* **Expense Tracking**: Yes (Users manually log flexible spending and recurring bills).
* **Budget Planning**: Yes (App calculates available surplus and suggests daily spending limits).
* **Debt Tracking**: Yes (Allows logging interest rates, minimum payments, and tracking balances).
* **Savings Goals**: Yes (Track emergency funds and custom goals).
* **AI Financial Explanations**: Yes (Conversational insights explaining budget health using Google Gemini).

---

## 2. Excluded Features (Not Implemented)
* **Direct Bank Connections**: No (We do not link to external bank accounts, APIs, or open-banking portals).
* **Money Transfer / Wallet**: No (We do not hold or transfer real currency).
* **Google Wallet Integration**: No (We do not offer Google Wallet passes or cards).
* **Lending / Loans / Investments**: No (We do not provide credit services, micro-loans, or stock/crypto trading).
